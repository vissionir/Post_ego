Hi. My name is Denis.

**Post-Ego** is a public research space.

It exists to unfold and preserve the result of a completed self-inquiry that lasted more than 12 years — not as a system or a teaching, but as a living network of connections.

Meaning here is not “written down” — it is held by connectedness.

The research began with fundamental questions — about self, knowledge, life direction, and the nature of things — and ended when those questions stopped demanding further search.

Over that time, more than 12,000 atomic theses, distinctions, and observations were recorded.

The result is not a system and does not belong to any single tradition. It formed across different domains — philosophy, psychology, attention practices, and lived experience — and cannot be honestly reduced to one doctrine without distortion.

There is no “new philosophy” here, and no path being offered to follow.

Over time one thing became clear: meaning is not contained in individual formulations — it arises from the links between them.

That’s why this site is structured as a network. There is no main text and no correct reading order. There are nodes and connections.

This is not an explanation and not an instruction manual — it is a way to hold and show the connectedness of what has already been understood.

---
title: 2nd-level imprint
---

A 2nd-level imprint is a particular kind of [[Imprint|imprint]]: a fixation of the only permissible mode of living in which the chosen way of being is experienced as the only possible and objectively real one; choice is not recognized, and configurations incompatible with it are repressed into the [[Shadow|shadow]]; work with such an imprint leads not to the correction of a reaction, but to a change in the structure of perception and the restoration of [[Free Choice|free choice]].

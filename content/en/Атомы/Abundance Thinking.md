---
title: Abundance Thinking
created: 2026-04-24T18:02:49+07:00
---

Abundance thinking is a mode of [[Thinking|thinking]] in which [[Reality|reality]] is perceived as a space of actually available resources, [[Proficitarity|possibilities]], and options for action, while the absence of what is desired is not experienced as [[Deficit|deficit]], does not limit [[Free Choice|free choice]], and forms an [[Abundant Environment|abundant environment]].

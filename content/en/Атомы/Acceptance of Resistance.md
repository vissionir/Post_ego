---
title: Acceptance of Resistance
created: 2026-05-20T16:21:53+07:00
---

Acceptance of resistance is the recognition of [[Resistance|resistance]] as a signal of [[Inner Conflict|inner conflict]], in which the struggle against the existence of resistance ceases and [[Choice|choice]] becomes possible between resolving the conflict, deciding not to act, and continuing the action through [[Self-Coercion|self-coercion]].

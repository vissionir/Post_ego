---
title: Acceptance of Suffering
created: 2026-09-04T15:18:37+07:00
---

Acceptance of suffering is a form of [[Acceptance|acceptance]] in which it is neither [[Suffering|suffering]] nor the striving to end it that ceases, but [[Clinging|clinging]] to the necessity of ending suffering, thereby ending the [[Cycle of Suffering|cycle of suffering]].

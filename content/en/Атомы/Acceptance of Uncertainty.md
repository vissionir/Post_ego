---
title: Acceptance of Uncertainty
---

Acceptance of uncertainty is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] according to which the absence of [[Predictability|predictability]] and the incompleteness of the [[Map|map]] are natural properties of [[Reality|reality]], conditioned by the [[Unpredictability of the world|unpredictability of the world]]; it serves as the basis for [[Tolerance of Uncertainty|tolerance of uncertainty]].

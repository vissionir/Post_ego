---
title: Acceptance of irreversibility
---

Acceptance of irreversibility is the [[Acceptance|acceptance]] that [[Irreversibility|irreversible]] changes are part of [[Impermanence|impermanence]]; it ends [[Clinging|clinging]] to the possibility of returning to a former [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]], thereby eliminating the [[Fear of irreversibility|fear of irreversibility]].

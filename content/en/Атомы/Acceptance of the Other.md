---
title: Acceptance of the Other
---

Acceptance of the Other is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] based on the [[I-Thou Relation|I-Thou relation]], in which the states, experiences, and forms of manifestation of the other are allowed without requiring their change, suppression, or conformity; it is the external expression of [[Self-Acceptance|self-acceptance]] and [[Freedom to Be Anything|freedom to be anything]].

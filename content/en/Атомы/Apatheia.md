Apatheia is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] arising from [[Acceptance of What Is Not in Our Power|acceptance of what is not in our power]], in which the subject preserves [[Inner Freedom|inner freedom]], stops spending inner resources on [[Resistance|resistance]] to [[What Is Not in Our Power|what is not in their power]], and acts within [[What Is in Our Power|what is in their power]].

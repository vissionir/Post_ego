---
title: Assemblage point
created: 2026-02-09T14:55:18+07:00
---

The assemblage point is the current configuration of perception through which experienced [[Reality|reality]] is assembled: the position from which [[Choice|choice]] and action are carried out; in [[Ego|ego]] it is fixed by [[Personality|personality]], and when [[Clinging|clinging]] ceases it becomes [[Unfixed Assemblage Point|unfixed]].

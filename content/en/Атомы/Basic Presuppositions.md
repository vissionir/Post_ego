---
title: Basic Presuppositions
---

Basic presuppositions are the foundational [[Presupposition|presuppositions]] underlying [[Basic Trust in the World|basic trust in the world]]; they include [[Unconditional Love|unconditional love]], [[Unconditional Worth|unconditional worth]], [[Unconditional Neededness|unconditional neededness]], [[Basic safety|basic safety]], [[Inner Freedom|inner freedom]], [[Self-Acceptance|self-acceptance]], [[Self-care|self-care]], [[Sufficiency|sufficiency]], [[Abundance|abundance]], [[Positive Orientation|positive orientation]], [[Self-Efficacy|self-efficacy]], [[Confidence|confidence]], [[Noncontingency|noncontingency]], and [[Basic peace|basic peace]].

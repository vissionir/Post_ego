title: Calming the Mind
created: 2026-09-25T07:24:47+07:00

Calming the mind is a process of weakening the spontaneous activity of the [[Mind|mind]] in which the [[Inner Dialogue|inner dialogue]] quiets, [[Mind Wandering|mind wandering]] weakens, and [[Papancha|papancha]] ceases, leading to [[Samatha|tranquility of mind]].

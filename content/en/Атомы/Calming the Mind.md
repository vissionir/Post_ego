Calming the mind is a process of weakening the spontaneous activity of the [[Mind|mind]] in which the [[Inner Dialogue|inner dialogue]] quiets, [[Mind Wandering|mind wandering]] and [[Papancha|papancha]] cease, leading to [[Serenity|serenity]].

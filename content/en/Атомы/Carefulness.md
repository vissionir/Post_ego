Carefulness is a form of [[Abundance Thinking|abundance thinking]] in which [[Interaction with the Environment|interaction with the environment]] proceeds from [[Sufficiency|sufficiency]] and [[Moderation|moderation]], not from the drive to extract the maximum; it is opposite to [[Extractivity|extractivity]].

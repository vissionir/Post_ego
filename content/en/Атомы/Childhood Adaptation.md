Childhood adaptation is a mode of behavioral regulation formed within the [[Childhood World Model|childhood world model]] as a response to objective dependence on an [[External Instance|external instance]]; without [[Initiation|initiation]], it persists into adulthood as an [[Adaptive Mismatch|adaptive mismatch]].

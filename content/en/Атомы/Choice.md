Choice is an act of the subject in which who they [[Assemblage point|are]] is determined, rather than what they will get; in [[Ego|ego]] it is not recognized and is reproduced automatically, while in [[Presence|presence]] it becomes available through [[Actualization of choice|actualization of choice]].

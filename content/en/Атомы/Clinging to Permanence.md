Clinging to Permanence is a form of [[Clinging|clinging]] based on the [[Illusion of Unchangingness|illusion of unchangingness]], in which the subject seeks to [[Status Quo Preservation|keep the current situation]] unchanged despite the [[Continuity of Happenings|continuity of what is happening]].

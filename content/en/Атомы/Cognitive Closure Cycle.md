The cognitive closure cycle is a [[Maintaining Cycle|maintaining cycle]] arising from [[Intolerance of Uncertainty|intolerance of uncertainty]], in which an answer temporarily relieves the tension of uncertainty, reinforcing the [[Drive for Cognitive Closure|drive]] to seek an answer to each new question.

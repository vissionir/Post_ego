Compensatory map expansion is a form of [[Control|control]] in which the expansion of the [[Map|map]] is used as a mechanism of [[Compensation|compensation]] for the absence of [[Basic safety|basic safety]]; it is opposite to [[Sufficiency of Knowledge|sufficiency of knowledge]].

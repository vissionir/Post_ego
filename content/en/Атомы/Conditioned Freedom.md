---
title: Conditioned Freedom
created: 2026-05-12T17:00:00+07:00
---

Conditioned freedom is a [[Illusion of the Mind|mind illusion]] in which [[Inner Freedom|inner freedom]] is mistakenly experienced as dependent on external conditions; it is dispelled through [[Acceptance of What Is Not in Our Power|acceptance of what is not in our power]] and leads to [[Apatheia|apatheia]].

---
title: Conditioned Freedom
---

Conditioned freedom is a [[Illusion of the Mind|mind illusion]] in which [[Inner Freedom|inner freedom]] is mistakenly experienced as dependent on external conditions; it sustains [[Resistance|resistance]] to [[What Is Not in Our Power|what is not in our power]] and is dispelled through [[Acceptance of What Is Not in Our Power|acceptance of what is not in our power]].

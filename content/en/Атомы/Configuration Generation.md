title: Configuration Generation
created: 2026-04-14T18:06:55+07:00
---

Configuration generation is the emergence of a new configuration of experience that was previously unavailable to the subject and was therefore perceived as absent from the [[Map|map]], in which new [[Submodal state|submodal states]] and [[Presupposition|presuppositions]] become available. It is carried out through [[Transition|transition]].

The danger of experience is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which the re-creation of a [[Submodal state|submodal state]] associated with a past threat is [[Threat verification|mistakenly]] experienced as encountering the threat itself again; it is dispelled through [[Dereification of Safety|dereification of safety]].

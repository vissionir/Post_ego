---
title: Deficit detection
---

Deficit detection is a function of the [[Mind|mind]] by which it proactively identifies the [[Insufficiency|insufficiency]] of conditions for ensuring safety, [[Predictability|predictability]], and the possibility of action, highlighting a possible [[Deficit|deficit]] as a priority signal, since [[Sufficiency|sufficiency]] does not require a response, while deficit points to potential [[Unsafety|unsafety]].

---
title: Depolarization
created: 2026-04-27T17:51:41+07:00
---

Depolarization is the effect of [[Integration|integration]], in which the opposite poles of a [[Dichotomy|dichotomy]] cease to be experienced as mutually exclusive and are recognized as parts of [[Selfhood|selfhood]], whereby [[Clinging|clinging]] to one of them ceases and [[Wholeness|wholeness]] is restored.

Despair is a [[Submodal state|submodal state]] arising during a [[Map rupture|map rupture]] caused by external circumstances, when the outcome sustained by [[Hope|hope]] is recognized as unattainable, while the absence of another admissible continuation in the [[Map|map]] leads to a forced [[Encounter with the unknown|encounter with the unknown]].

Dharma is a description of [[Reality|reality]] that is as accurate as possible and a set of principles, methods, and [[Presupposition|presuppositions]] that create the conditions for [[Direct Seeing|seeing reality directly]] and for recognizing and eliminating [[Illusion of the Mind|illusions of the mind]].

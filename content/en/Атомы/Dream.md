---
title: Dream
---

A dream is a [[Fantasy|fantasy]] that became a stable preference and has a vector of realization in [[Reality|reality]], allowing at least one implementation mechanism without violating known limits of the world; it holds a future form of realization as a separate image until the [[Mission|mission]] has become current [[Intention|intention]]. Under [[Mission Congruence|mission congruence]], a dream loses this function because direction ceases to be an image of the future and begins to manifest in action.

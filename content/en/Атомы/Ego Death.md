Ego Death is a passage through [[Fear of Death|fear of death]] in which [[Disidentification|disidentification]] occurs, the need to [[Clinging to the I|cling to "I"]] disappears, and [[Anatta|anatta]] is recognized; it cannot be carried out by the [[Mind|mind]].

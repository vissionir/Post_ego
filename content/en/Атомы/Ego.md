The ego is a stable structure of 'I' arising through [[Identification|identification]] with the [[Self-Model|self-model]]. Within it, the part of [[Selfhood|selfhood]] singled out in [[The conscious|the conscious]] is experienced as 'I', while the part displaced into the [[Shadow|shadow]] is experienced as 'not I'.

---
title: Emergence of the Dharma
created: 2026-09-25T07:24:47+07:00
---

Emergence of the Dharma is the arising, from the interconnections among [[Dharma|Dharma's]] propositions, of a holistic understanding unavailable when those propositions are considered separately and leading to [[Realization of the Dharma|realization of the Dharma]].

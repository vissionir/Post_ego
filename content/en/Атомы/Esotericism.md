---
title: Esotericism
---

Esotericism is a set of beliefs and practices based on [[Magical thinking|magical thinking]] and [[Mind hallucination|mind hallucinations]], attributing to [[Reality|reality]] hidden forces, intentions, and laws that cannot be verified through direct experience; esotericism includes notions of “energies,” “vibrations,” karma, destiny, “signs of the Universe,” astral influences, and spiritual entities, and esotericists are [[State addiction|state addicts]] and belong to the category of [[The lost|the lost]].

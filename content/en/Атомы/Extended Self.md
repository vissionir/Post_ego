---
title: Extended Self
created: 2026-05-11T19:30:00+07:00
---

The extended self is a form of [[Clinging|clinging]] in which the [[Ego|ego]] includes external objects, roles, places, people, ideas, or experience within the boundaries of the "I" through the experience of "mine"; it arises on the basis of the [[Illusion of Duality|illusion of duality]].

Extractivity is a form of [[Scarcity mindset|scarcity mindset]] in which the drive to extract the maximum from [[Interaction with the Environment|interaction with the environment]] functions as a way of compensating for [[Deficit|deficit]]; it is opposite to [[Carefulness|carefulness]].

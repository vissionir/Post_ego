False responsibility is a form of [[Hyper-Responsibility|hyper-responsibility]] in which the subject assumes responsibility for another person’s [[Submodal state|state]], [[Well-Being|well-being]], [[Suffering|suffering]], decisions, and actions, as well as the consequences of those decisions and actions.

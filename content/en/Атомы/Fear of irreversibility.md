Fear of irreversibility is the [[Fear|fear]] of the [[Irreversibility|irreversibility]] of change, based on [[Stability of the I|stability of the I]], in which an [[Irreversible shift|irreversible shift]] is experienced as a threat of the disappearance of the [[Ego|current form of "I"]] and leads to holding the current [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]].

---
title: Fear of irreversibility
---

Fear of irreversibility is the [[Fear|fear]] of the [[Irreversibility|irreversibility]] of change, based on [[Ego Homeostasis|ego homeostasis]], in which an [[Irreversible shift|irreversible shift]] is experienced as a threat to the stability of the [[Ego|ego]] and leads to holding the current [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]].

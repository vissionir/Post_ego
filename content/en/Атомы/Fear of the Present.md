Fear of the present is [[Fear|fear]] in which the immediate experience of "here-now" is experienced as [[Unsafety|unsafe]], because [[Reality|reality]] is perceived as [[Hostile Environment|hostile]], and its [[Unpredictability of the world|unpredictability]] causes [[Intolerance of Uncertainty|intolerance of uncertainty]].

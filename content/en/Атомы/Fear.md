---
title: Fear
---

Fear is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusory]] experience of unsafety arising in the [[Mind|mind]] and not directly connected with real danger. Fear always has its source in an [[Imprint|imprint]] and does not arise from direct perception of [[Reality|reality]]. In [[Carlos Castaneda|Carlos Castaneda]], fear is described as the first of the four enemies of the [[Man of knowledge|man of knowledge]].

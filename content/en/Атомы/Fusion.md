---
title: Fusion
created: 2026-04-29T16:44:01+07:00
---

Fusion is a mode of experience in which the separate existence of different aspects of experience is not allowed, making [[Distinction|distinction]] between them unavailable, so that they are experienced as one indistinguishable whole. In a particular case, fusion may be reinforced by a [[2nd-level imprint|2nd-level imprint]].

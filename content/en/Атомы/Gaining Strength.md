Gaining strength is an [[Irreversible shift|irreversible shift]] from [[Powerlessness|powerlessness]] to [[Power|strength]], in which a subject discovers [[Personal power|their own capacity to influence what happens]] and shifts from [[Reactive mode of existence|reacting]] to [[Agency|affecting reality]] as their primary way of interacting with it.

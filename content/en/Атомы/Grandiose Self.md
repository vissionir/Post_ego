---
title: Grandiose Self
created: 2026-06-10T19:45:55+07:00
---

The grandiose self is a form of the [[Sense of Self-Importance|sense of self-importance]] in which the image of I is experienced through exceptionality, superiority, or the right to special treatment; it is opposite to the [[Insignificant Self|insignificant self]].

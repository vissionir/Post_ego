title: Hyper-Responsibility

Hyper-responsibility is an [[Anxiety|anxious]] form of [[Responsibility|responsibility]], arising on the basis of the [[Illusion of Control|illusion of control]], in which the subject mistakenly includes in their zone of responsibility what lies beyond [[What Is in Our Power|their power]].

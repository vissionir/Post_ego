title: Ideal Self
created: 2026-04-17T15:33:05+07:00

The ideal self is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] based on the [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion]] that there exists a fixed "correct" version of the subject; relative to it, the current state is experienced as [[Insufficiency|insufficient]]. It is dispelled through [[Unconditional Love|unconditional love]].

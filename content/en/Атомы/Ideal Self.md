---
title: Ideal Self
---

The ideal self is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] formed in the absence of the experience of [[Unconditional Love|unconditional love]] and based on the [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion]] that there exists a fixed "correct" version of the subject, conformity to which is a condition of [[Love|love]]; relative to it, the current state is experienced as [[Insufficiency|insufficient]]. It is dispelled through [[Unconditional Love|unconditional love]].

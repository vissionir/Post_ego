Illusion of coercion is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which [[Self-Coercion|self-coercion]] is experienced as the only way to continue action in the presence of [[Resistance|resistance]]; it is dispelled through [[Acceptance of Resistance|acceptance of resistance]].

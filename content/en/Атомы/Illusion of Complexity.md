---
title: Illusion of Complexity
---

Illusion of complexity is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which a forecast of the high cost of an action formed in [[Prospection|prospection]] gives rise to the [[Aversiveness|aversiveness]] of its experience, which is mistakenly taken as an intrinsic property of the action; it is dispelled through [[Reconfiguration of Experience|reconfiguration of experience]].

Illusion of control is a [[Illusion of the Mind|mind illusion]] in which [[Control|control]] is experienced as a way to make [[Reality|reality]] sufficiently [[Predictability|predictable]] under conditions of [[Unpredictability of the world|the unpredictability of the world]]; it sustains [[Power of the mind|the power of the mind]], and its dispelling leads to [[Non-doing|non-doing]].

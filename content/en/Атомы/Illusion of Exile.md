The illusion of exile is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which rejection by one group is [[Overgeneralization|mistakenly]] experienced as the loss of all [[Social Safety|social safety]], despite the [[Replaceability of the Social Environment|availability of alternatives]].

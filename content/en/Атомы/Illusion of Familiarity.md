The illusion of familiarity is a [[Cognitive distortion|cognitive distortion]] in which [[Status Quo Preservation|preserving the current situation]] is erroneously perceived as choosing the [[Familiarity of What Is Happening|known present]], although the choice is actually between two uncertain versions of the future.

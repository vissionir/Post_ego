---
title: Illusion of Freedom
created: 2026-05-06T14:21:15+07:00

The illusion of freedom is a [[Illusion of the Mind|mind illusion]] in which an [[Egregore|egregore]] is mistakenly taken to be the originally given order of [[Reality|reality]], although it is only a collectively sustained model of existence; its dissolution leads to [[External Freedom|external freedom]].

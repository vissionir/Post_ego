---
title: Illusion of Immortality
---

The illusion of immortality is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which the [[Finitude of Life|finitude of one's own life]] is not taken into account in experience and choice, as a result of which the subject acts as though the time of their life were unlimited; it is dispelled through the [[Acceptance of Mortality|acceptance of mortality]].

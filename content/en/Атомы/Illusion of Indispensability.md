---
title: Illusion of Indispensability
created: 2026-05-12T15:09:59+07:00
---

The illusion of indispensability is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of mind]] arising from [[Hyper-Responsibility|hyper-responsibility]], in which the subject mistakenly experiences themselves as a necessary condition for understanding, organizing, or carrying out an action; its dissolution makes [[Management|management]] available.

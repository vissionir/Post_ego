---
title: Illusion of Insufficiency
---

The illusion of insufficiency is a [[Illusion of the Mind|mind illusion]] in which [[Insufficiency|insufficiency]] identified by [[Deficit detection|deficit detection]] is not recognized as a result of the work of the [[Mind|mind]] and is experienced as a property of [[Reality|reality]] itself; it is the basis of [[Scarcity mindset|deficit thinking]], and its dissolution leads to [[Sobriety|sobriety]].

The illusion of omnipotence is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which a desired outcome is experienced as available in advance, regardless of the [[Acceptance of Limitations|limits of one's abilities]] and the [[Unpredictability of the world|unpredictability of the world]].

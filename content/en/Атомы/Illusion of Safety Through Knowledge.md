The illusion of safety through knowledge is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] rooted in [[Intolerance of Uncertainty|intolerance of uncertainty]], in which knowledge, understanding, or completeness of the [[Map|map]] is mistakenly experienced as a condition of [[Basic safety|basic safety]].

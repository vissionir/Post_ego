---
title: Illusion of Safety Through Understanding
---

The illusion of safety through understanding is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] rooted in [[Intolerance of Uncertainty|intolerance of uncertainty]], in which knowledge, understanding, or the completeness of the [[Map|map]] is mistakenly experienced as a condition of [[Basic safety|basic safety]], leading to [[Compensatory Map Expansion|compensatory expansion of the map]].

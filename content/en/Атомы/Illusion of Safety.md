---
title: Illusion of Safety
---

Illusion of safety is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] based on the false identity "predictability = safety", as a result of which [[Basic safety|basic safety]] begins to be experienced as dependent on [[Predictability|predictability]]; it is the basis of [[Power of the mind|the power of the mind]], and the dispelling of this illusion leads to [[Delegitimation of the Mind|delegitimation of the mind]].

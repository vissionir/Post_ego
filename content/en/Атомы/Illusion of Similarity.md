---
title: Illusion of Similarity
created: 2026-08-29T17:07:13+07:00
---

The illusion of similarity is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] based on [[False Consensus|false consensus]], in which another person's perception, interpretations, desires, and choices are assumed by default to be similar to one's own; it is dispelled through [[People Are Different|recognizing differences in people's psychic organization]].

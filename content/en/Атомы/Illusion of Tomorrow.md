---
title: Illusion of Tomorrow
---

The illusion of tomorrow is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which the continuation of one's own life is [[Safety Limit|mistakenly]] experienced as guaranteed, while [[Predictability|predictability]] is [[Illusion of Safety|mistakenly]] taken as a way to ensure it; it is dispelled through [[Readiness for Death|readiness for death]].

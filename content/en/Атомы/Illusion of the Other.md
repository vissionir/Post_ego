The illusion of the other is a [[Illusion of the Mind|mind illusion]] in which a model of another person's inner world, constructed by the [[Mind|mind]], is taken to be reality, although the subject is [[There are no people|factually given]] only that person's manifestations in [[VAK|VAK]]. The dissolution of this illusion leads to the de-actualization of the [[Inner pack|inner pack]] and leaves the [[Second position|second position]] only as a method, not as a mode of perception.

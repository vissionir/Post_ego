---
title: Illusory Search for a Solution
created: 2026-09-04T15:18:37+07:00
---

An illusory search for a solution is a form of [[Self-Deception|self-deception]] in which the subject, having [[Recognition of the Map's Limit|recognized the limit of the current map]], nevertheless continues its [[Map Refinement|refinement]], taking refinement for progress toward a solution, although the process’s [[Secondary gain|unconscious goal]] is to [[Status Quo Preservation|preserve the current condition]].

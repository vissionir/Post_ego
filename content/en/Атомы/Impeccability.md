---
title: Impeccability
created: 2026-02-17T16:40:53+07:00
---
Impeccability is a way of living and acting with awareness of the [[Controlled folly|conditionality of what is happening]], with full expenditure of energy in every step, without [[Self-Pity|self-pity]] and [[Sense of Self-Importance|sense of self-importance]], taking decisions as final and acting as if each act were the last.

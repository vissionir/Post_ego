---
title: Impermanence
created: 2026-03-15T16:48:01+07:00
---

Impermanence is the principle that every conditioned phenomenon is a temporary configuration of process: states, events, relationships, and [[Map|maps of perception]] arise, change, and cease. Accepting this fact ends the demand that the world be stable and creates an inner consent to live inside [[Unpredictability of the world|continuous change]].

Impermanence is the principle that every conditioned phenomenon is a temporary configuration of process: states, events, relationships, and [[Map|maps of perception]] arise, change, and cease, so none of their current states is final. Accepting this fact ends the demand that [[Stability of Reality|the world be stable]] and creates an inner consent to live inside [[Continuity of Happenings|continuous change]].

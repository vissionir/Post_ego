title: Imprint Complex
created: 2026-08-29T17:07:13+07:00
---

An imprint complex is a system of [[Imprint|imprints]] supporting a single pattern of experience or behavior. If the complex is organized by one leading imprint, the pattern disappears after that imprint is eliminated; if it is organized by several independent imprints, the pattern disappears only after each of them is eliminated.

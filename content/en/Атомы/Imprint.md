---
title: Imprint
created: 2026-02-06T14:20:39+07:00
---

An imprint is a pattern of reaction fixed in past experience that is automatically activated in the present outside direct perception of [[Reality|reality]]; in the research, [[1st-level imprint|1st-level imprints]] and [[2nd-level imprint|2nd-level imprints]] are distinguished, and [[Psychotherapy|psychotherapy]] is considered a process of identifying and eliminating imprints.

Influence is a change in another's [[Frame|frame]] through [[Involvement|involvement]], leading to a restructuring of their state, interpretation, and behavior, in which the corresponding frame becomes integrated into their [[Map|map]] and begins to determine that map's interpretation; it is a realization of [[Agency|agency]].

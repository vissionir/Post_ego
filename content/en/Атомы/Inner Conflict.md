title: Inner Conflict
created: 2026-04-13T14:43:02+07:00
---

Inner conflict is a contradiction within the [[Mind|mind]] arising from the [[Dichotomy|dichotomous]] division of what is happening into incompatible alternatives. Instrumentally it is removed in [[Psychotherapy|psychotherapy]], and fundamentally it is resolved through [[Acceptance|acceptance]], direct experience of [[Nonduality|nonduality]] and [[Suchness|thusness]].

title: Inner Conflict
created: 2026-04-13T14:43:02+07:00
---

Inner conflict is a contradiction within the [[Mind|mind]] arising from the [[Dichotomy|dichotomous]] division of what is happening into incompatible alternatives. It is eliminated through [[Psychotherapy|psychotherapy]] at an instrumental level and resolved through [[Self-Acceptance|self-acceptance]] at a fundamental level.

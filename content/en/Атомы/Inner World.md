The inner world is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which distinguishable [[Content of Experience|contents]] of a single [[Field of Experience|field of experience]] are perceived as [[Duality|divided]] into “internal” and “external,” as a result of which content of the [[Mind|mind]] is mistakenly experienced as separate from the other contents and [[Power of the mind|dominant]] over them.

---
title: Insignificant Self
created: 2026-06-10T19:45:55+07:00
---

The insignificant self is a form of the [[Sense of Self-Importance|sense of self-importance]] in which the image of I is experienced through [[Insufficiency|insufficiency]], defectiveness, or insignificance; it is opposite to the [[Grandiose Self|grandiose self]].

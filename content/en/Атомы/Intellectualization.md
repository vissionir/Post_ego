---
title: Intellectualization
---

Intellectualization is a form of psychological defense in which the [[Mind|mind]] replaces [[Contact with Experience|contact with experience]] with explanation, analysis, or construction of a [[Map|map]], preserving [[Control|control]] and [[Power of the mind|power of the mind]], while avoiding the living-through of the [[Submodal state|state]] necessary for [[Integration|integration]].

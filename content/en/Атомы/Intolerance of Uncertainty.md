---
title: Intolerance of Uncertainty
---

Intolerance of uncertainty is a manifestation of [[Anxiety|anxiety]] in which the absence of future [[Predictability|predictability]] is experienced as a threat to [[Basic safety|basic safety]], as a result of which the [[Mind|mind]] either launches [[Control|control]] to restore certainty or [[Escapism|escapism]] for [[Avoidance|avoiding]] the experience of uncertainty.

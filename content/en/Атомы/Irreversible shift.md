---
title: Irreversible shift
created: 2026-05-03T01:33:41+07:00
---

An irreversible shift is a [[Neurocognitive Reorganization|neurocognitive reorganization]] in which the former way of [[Interpretation|interpreting]] experience becomes unavailable; it may be accompanied by a [[Map rupture|map rupture]], in which the very [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]] from which what is happening is perceived changes.

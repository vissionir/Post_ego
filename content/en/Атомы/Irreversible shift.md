---
title: Irreversible shift
created: 2026-05-03T01:33:41+07:00
---

An irreversible shift is the result of a [[Transition|transition]] after which return to the former way of [[Interpretation|interpreting]] experience becomes impossible due to [[Neurocognitive Reorganization|neurocognitive reorganization]]; it may be accompanied by a [[Map rupture|map rupture]], in which the very [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]] from which what is happening is perceived changes.

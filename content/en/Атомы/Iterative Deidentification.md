Iterative Deidentification is a method of [[Deidentification|deidentification]] in which the subject repeatedly [[Inhibition|inhibits]] following the [[Inner Dialogue|inner dialogue]] and, through [[VAK verification|VAK verification]], observes that action continues without management by the [[Mind|mind]].

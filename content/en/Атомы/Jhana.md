Jhana is a form of [[Samadhi|meditative concentration]] characterized by [[Effortlessness|effortless]] stability of attention and a change in the character of [[Field of Experience|experience]] — from rapture and pleasure to [[Upekkha|equanimity]] and purity of mindfulness.

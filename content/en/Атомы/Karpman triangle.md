---
title: Karpman triangle
---

The Karpman triangle is a destructive model of interaction in which [[Inner Conflict|conflict]] is realized through switching between the roles of [[Self-Pity|victim]], [[Rescuing|rescuer]], and [[Aggression|persecutor]] when identified with one of them; the triangle exists as long as identification with the current role remains and self-sustains through switching between roles.

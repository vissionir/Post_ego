---
title: Karpman triangle
created: 2026-04-19T14:18:06+07:00
---

The Karpman triangle is a destructive model of interaction in which [[Inner Conflict|conflict]] is enacted through switching between the roles of [[Self-Pity|victim]], [[Rescuing|rescuer]], and [[Aggression|persecutor]] while the subject is [[Identification|identified]] with one of them; the triangle exists as long as identification with the current role persists and sustains itself through switching between roles.

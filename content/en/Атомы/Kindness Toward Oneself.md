Kindness toward oneself is a principle for how one relates to oneself that brings together [[Metta|mettā]], [[Compassion|karuṇā]], [[Sympathetic Joy|muditā]], [[Upekkha|upekkhā]], and [[Ahimsa|ahiṃsā]] and calls for refraining from [[Self-Coercion|self-coercion]] and [[Self-Punishment|self-punishment]].

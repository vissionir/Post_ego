Life in the unknown is a condition arising from [[Stopping the world|stopping the world]], in which [[Acceptance of Uncertainty|uncertainty]] and [[Impermanence|changeability]] are accepted as natural properties of [[Reality|reality]], [[Personal power|personal power]] makes action possible, [[Intention|intention]] sets direction, and [[Impeccability|impeccability]] determines how one acts.

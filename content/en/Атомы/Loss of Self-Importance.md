Loss of self-importance is the [[Letting Go|cessation]] of [[Sense of Self-Importance|constant concern with oneself]], in which the [[Indulgence|fixation of attention on oneself]] ceases, leading to [[Attention Reorientation|attention reorientation]] and freeing [[Personal power|personal power]].

---
title: Lost Profit
---

Lost profit is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] arising from [[Scarcity mindset|scarcity mindset]], in which an alternative result modeled by [[Counterfactual Thinking|counterfactual thinking]] is taken as a really lost resource, creating an experience of [[Deficit|deficit]]; it is dispelled through [[Proficitarity|proficitarity]].

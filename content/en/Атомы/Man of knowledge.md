---
title: Man of knowledge
---

According to [[Carlos Castaneda|Carlos Castaneda]], a man of knowledge is one who was not stopped by fear ([[The first enemy of a man of knowledge|the first enemy]]), not blinded by clarity ([[The second enemy of a man of knowledge|the second enemy]]), not enslaved by power ([[The third enemy of a man of knowledge|the third enemy]]), and continues the path despite old age ([[The fourth enemy of a man of knowledge|the fourth enemy]]).

---
title: Materiality of Thought
created: 2026-08-05T00:55:22+07:00
---

Materiality of thought is the capacity of a [[Thought|thought]] to indirectly alter [[Reality|reality]] by changing a [[Submodal state|submodal state]], which determines the subject's behavior through physiological regulation and thereby alters the [[Environment|environment]] through their actions.

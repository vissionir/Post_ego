---
title: Narcissistic Introjection
created: 2026-08-29T17:07:13+07:00

Narcissistic introjection is a form of [[Parental Introjection|parental introjection]] in which [[Narcissistic Extension|narcissistic extension]] is assimilated by the child as the norm of close relationships and is subsequently [[Reproduction of the Parental Model|reproduced]] in relationships with significant others.

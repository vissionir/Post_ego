---
title: Non-Appropriation of Thoughts
created: 2026-10-02T11:05:32+07:00
---

Non-appropriation of thoughts is a position arising as a result of [[Dereification of Inner Dialogue|dereification of inner dialogue]], in which arising [[Thought|thoughts]] cease to be experienced as [[Extended Self|"mine"]] and their [[Clinging|appropriation]] ceases.

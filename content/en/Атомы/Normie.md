A normie is a person for whom [[Thinking|thinking]], reactions, and interpretations of [[Mind|the mind]] are experienced as “I” and as [[Reality|reality]] itself; as a result, they live in [[Mind automatism|automatisms]] and their [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]] remains fixed, because reality matches expectations well enough and [[Prediction error|prediction error]] remains minimal.

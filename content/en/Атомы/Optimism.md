---
title: Optimism
---
Optimism is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] of [[Basic Trust in the World|basic trust in the world]] with regard to the future, in which the future is by default interpreted as non-threatening to existence and favorable; it is [[Dichotomy|dichotomous]] to [[Pessimism|pessimism]] and necessary for a stable [[Author position|author position]].

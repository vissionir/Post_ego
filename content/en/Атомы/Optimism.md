created: 2026-02-15T13:15:13+07:00

Optimism is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] regarding the future, in which it is interpreted by default as non-threatening to existence and favorable; it forms a [[Dichotomy|dichotomy]] with [[Pessimism|pessimism]] and is necessary for a stable [[Author position|author position]].

---
title: Overcoming Fear of Death
---

Overcoming fear of death is an [[Irreversible shift|irreversible shift]] arising as a result of [[Ego Death|ego death]], in which the [[Fear of Death|fear of death]] is recognized as the [[Ego|ego's]] fear of its own disappearance, and [[Death|death]] as the cessation of experience, as a result of which the [[Search for Certainty|search for certainty]] loses its basis and ceases.

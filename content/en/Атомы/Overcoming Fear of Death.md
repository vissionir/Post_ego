---
title: Overcoming Fear of Death
---

Overcoming fear of death is an [[Irreversible shift|irreversible shift]] arising as a result of [[Ego Death|ego death]], in which the [[Fear of Death|fear of death]] is recognized as the [[Ego|ego's]] fear of its own disappearance and loses its basis, as a result of which the [[Search for Certainty|search for certainty]] ceases.

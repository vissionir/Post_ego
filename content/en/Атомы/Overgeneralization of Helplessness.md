---
title: Overgeneralization of Helplessness
created: 2026-09-01T11:26:28+07:00
---

Overgeneralization of helplessness is an [[Overgeneralization|overgeneralization]] in which the [[What Is Not in Our Power|inability to fulfill a particular desire]] is mistakenly interpreted as a loss of [[Functional Independence|functional independence]] and the capacity to act in general.

Parentification is a [[2nd-level imprint|2nd-level imprint]] arising when a child is assigned age-inappropriate responsibility for their parents’ state and life decisions, in which [[False Responsibility|assuming responsibility for another]] is experienced as the only permissible way to ensure [[Basic safety|safety]].

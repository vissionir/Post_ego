Paternalism is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which the subject claims the right to determine another person’s [[Well-Being|well-being]], without recognizing that person’s [[Sovereignty|sovereignty]], under the pretext of caring for their good, with the [[Secondary gain|unrecognized goal]] of eliminating the subject’s own [[Anxiety|anxiety]].

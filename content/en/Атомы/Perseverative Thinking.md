---
title: Perseverative Thinking
---

Perseverative thinking is a dysfunctional form of [[Iterative Modeling|iterative modeling]] arising from [[Need for Cognitive Closure|the need for cognitive closure]], in which the [[Mind|mind]] repeatedly returns to a threat, problem, or mistake in order to reduce [[Anxiety|anxiety]], but thereby enters a [[Maintaining Cycle|maintaining cycle]] that preserves that anxiety.

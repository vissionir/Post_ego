title: "Pessimism"
created: 2026-02-15T13:15:13+07:00

Pessimism is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] regarding the future, in which it is interpreted by default as threatening existence; it forms a [[Dichotomy|dichotomy]] with [[Optimism|optimism]] and triggers the [[Reactive mode of existence|reactive mode of existence]].

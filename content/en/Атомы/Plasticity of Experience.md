---
title: Plasticity of Experience
created: 2026-06-01T19:37:26+07:00
---

Plasticity of experience is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] according to which one's relation to any phenomenon is not a property of the phenomenon itself, but is a [[Submodal state|submodal state]], the [[Reconfiguration of Experience|change]] of which changes one's relation to that phenomenon.

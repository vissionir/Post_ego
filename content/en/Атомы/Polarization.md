Polarization is a form of [[Clinging|clinging]] to experience, expressed as [[Attraction|attraction]] to one pole of a [[Dichotomy|dichotomy]] and [[Aversion|aversion]] to the other, which ceases as a result of [[Depolarization|depolarization]].

---
title: Posthumanism
---
Posthumanism is a philosophical position that treats the human not as a [[De-anthropocentrization|privileged center of the world]] but as one process within a broader system of life. It shifts attention from “the human as measure” to networks of relations between organisms, environments, technologies, and forms of agency, where the human is only a partial mode. In this view, the boundaries of subjecthood and responsibility require reassembly: action and knowledge emerge in distributed circuits, not in an autonomous “I”.

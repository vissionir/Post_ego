---
title: Preverbal Imprint
---

Preverbal imprint is an [[Imprint|imprint]] formed before full understanding of the meaning of words and fixed in [[Implicit Memory|implicit memory]] not through verbal content, but through [[VAK|VAK]] context: tone, facial expression, volume, the experience of unsafety, threat, or rejection; access to it and its processing occur at the level of the [[Submodal state|submodal state]], rather than through semantic recollection.

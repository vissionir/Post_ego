title: Qualia
created: 2026-03-05T20:21:28+07:00
---
Qualia are minimal units of subjective experience — concrete elements of what is felt: a color sensation, a sound timbre, a taste, a tactile pressure. Qualia arise within perceptual modalities (visual, auditory, kinesthetic, etc.). Each quale has a set of parameters described as [[Submodalities|submodalities]].

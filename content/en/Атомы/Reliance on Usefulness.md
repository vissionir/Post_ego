Reliance on usefulness is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which usefulness to others is mistakenly experienced as a necessary condition of one's own [[Conditioned Worth|worth]], [[Conditional Neededness|neededness]], or being [[Love|loved]].

Repression is a consequence of [[Denial|denial]] in which parts of the psyche within [[Selfhood|selfhood]] that are incompatible with the self-image are excluded from the [[The conscious|conscious]] and cease to be recognized as "one's own", forming the [[Shadow|shadow]].

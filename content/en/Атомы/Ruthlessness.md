Ruthlessness is a [[Counter-Presupposition|counter-presupposition]] in which the subject does not use [[Self-Pity|self-pity]] as a way of interpreting [[Reality|what is happening]], thereby depriving the [[Sense of Self-Importance|sense of self-importance]] of its basis.

Sabai-sabai is a way of living [[Reality|reality]], accompanied by an experience of [[Well-Being|well-being]] and characterized by inner relaxation, comfort, lightness, [[Peace|peace]], [[Serenity|serenity]], [[Unforcedness|unforcedness]], [[Effortlessness|effortlessness]], and the absence of [[Urgency|urgency]].

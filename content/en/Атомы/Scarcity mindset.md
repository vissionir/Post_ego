---
title: Scarcity mindset
---
Scarcity mindset is a mode of [[Thinking|thinking]] in which the world, resources, possibilities, and the subject themselves are by default experienced as [[Insufficiency|insufficient]], and the absence of the desired is interpreted as a [[Deficit|deficit]], which triggers [[Compensation|compensation]] and limits [[Free Choice|free choice]]; it is opposite to [[Abundance Thinking|abundance mindset]].

---
title: Scarcity mindset
---

Scarcity mindset is a mode of [[Thinking|thinking]] in which resources are by default experienced as [[Insufficiency|insufficient]], possibilities as unique and irreplaceable, and the absence of the desired is interpreted as a [[Deficit|deficit]], which triggers [[Compensation|compensation]], limits [[Free Choice|free choice]], and forms a [[Deficit Environment|deficit environment]]; it is opposite to [[Abundance Thinking|abundance mindset]].

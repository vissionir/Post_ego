---
title: Search for Certainty
---

The search for certainty is a [[Maintaining Cycle|maintaining cycle]] triggered by the [[Fear of Death|fear of death]] and based on the [[Illusion of Safety|illusion of safety]], in which the [[Mind|mind]], through [[Predictive modeling|predictive modeling]], seeks to reduce the uncertainty of the future to zero in order to guarantee [[Survival|the continuation of life]], although [[Safety Limit|such a guarantee is fundamentally unattainable]].

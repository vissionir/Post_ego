---
title: Secular Buddhism
created: 2026-02-09T14:55:53+07:00
---

Secular Buddhism is an approach to the study of [[Dharma|dharma]], based on an interpretation of Buddhism that excludes metaphysical and [[Esotericism|esoteric]] claims and is focused on the direct investigation of experience, the recognition of [[Presence|presence]], and the cessation of [[Identification|identification]].

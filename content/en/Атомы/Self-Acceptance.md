Self-acceptance is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] according to which any inner experience is fully [[Acceptance|accepted]] without requiring its change, suppression, or conformity, thereby becoming available for [[Integration|integration]], which leads to [[Wholeness|wholeness]].

title: Self-Acceptance
created: 2026-04-17T14:20:42+07:00

Self-acceptance is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] according to which any of the subject's own experiences, states, and aspects are [[Acceptance|accepted]] without requiring their change, suppression, or conformity; it creates conditions for [[Integration|integration]] and leads to [[Wholeness|wholeness]].

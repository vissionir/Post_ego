---
title: Self-Deception
created: 2026-08-05T00:55:22+07:00
---

Self-deception is a form of [[Avoidance|avoidance]] of [[Incongruence|incongruence]] in which a subject preserves a declarative representation of themselves by evading [[VAK verification|verification]] or ignoring results that contradict it, with the [[Secondary gain|unrecognized goal]] of protecting their [[Sense of Self-Importance|sense of self-importance]].

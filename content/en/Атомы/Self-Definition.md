Self-definition is a form of [[Self-Determination|self-determination]] in which the subject [[Free Choice|freely]] [[Author position|creates]] and [[Affirmation|affirms]] the [[Frame|frames]] of their own life instead of [[Conformism|conformist]] [[Involvement|involvement]] in those of others.

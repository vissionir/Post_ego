Self-handicapping is a behavioral consequence of [[Self-Deception|self-deception]] in which a subject obstructs the achievement of their own [[Goal|goals]] through [[Procrastination|procrastination]], [[Substitute Action|substitute actions]], an [[Illusory Search for a Solution|illusory search for a solution]], and the [[Illusion of Progress|illusion of progress]], thereby [[Avoidance|avoiding]] possible confirmation of their inability to achieve those goals.

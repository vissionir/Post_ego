Self-model decentration is an [[Irreversible shift|irreversible shift]] arising from [[Deidentification of the Self-Model|deidentification of the self-model]], in which the [[Self-Model|self-model]] ceases to be experienced as a separate perceiving 'I' and the center of what is happening, while perception from it becomes [[Aperspectival Shift|just one of the possible]] [[Assemblage point|configurations of perception]].

Self-reflection is a form of the [[Sense of Self-Importance|sense of self-importance]] in which one's [[Self-Model|representation of oneself]] becomes the center of perception, as a result of which the subject perceives [[Reality|what happens]] not [[Direct Seeing|directly]], but through its continuous [[Interpretation|interpretation]] in relation to oneself.

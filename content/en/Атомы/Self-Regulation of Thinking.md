Self-regulation of thinking is a form of [[Power over the Mind|power over the mind]] in which [[Noticing|noticing]] a [[Thought|thought]] opens the possibility of directing its further unfolding, continuing to think it through, or ending it, [[Free Choice|without making any of these options obligatory]].

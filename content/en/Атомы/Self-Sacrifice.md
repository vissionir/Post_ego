title: Self-Sacrifice

Self-sacrifice is a form of [[Hyper-Responsibility|hyper-responsibility]] in which, due to the [[Second-Position Automatism|automatic activation of the second position]], the subject [[Illusion of the Other|mistakenly]] assumes [[Emotional Responsibility|responsibility for the state of the other]] and acts to regulate it at the cost of their own needs, desires, and interests.

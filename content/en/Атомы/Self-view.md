title: Self-view
created: 2026-06-05T18:41:02+07:00

Self-view is an [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion of the mind]] in which [[Impermanence|arising and passing]] contents of experience are mistakenly taken as [[Ego|"I"]] or [[Extended Self|"mine"]]; it is dispelled through [[Anatta|anatta]].

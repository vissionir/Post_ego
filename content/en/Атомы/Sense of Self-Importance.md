---
title: Sense of Self-Importance
---

The sense of self-importance is a process of [[Clinging|clinging]] to an image of oneself, based on the [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion]] of the existence of a permanent "[[Ego|I]]" to which the [[Mind|mind]] by default attributes [[Importance|importance]]; as a result, what is happening is interpreted through this center, which fixes the [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]].

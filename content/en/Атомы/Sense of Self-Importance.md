---
title: Sense of Self-Importance
---

The sense of self-importance is a process of [[Clinging|clinging]] to an image of oneself, based on the [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion]] of a permanent '[[Ego|I]]' to which the [[Mind|mind]] assigns importance by default. As a result, what happens is interpreted through this center, fixing the [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]].

---
title: Sense of Self-Importance
---

The sense of self-importance is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] based on the [[Illusion of the Mind|illusion]] of the existence of a permanent "[[Ego|I]]" to which the [[Mind|mind]] by default attributes [[Importance|importance]]; as a result, what is happening is interpreted through this center, which fixes the [[Assemblage point|assembly point]].

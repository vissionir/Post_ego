title: Separation
created: 2026-08-29T17:07:13+07:00
---

Separation is an act of [[Differentiation|differentiating]] oneself and the other, in which the other ceases to be experienced as part of oneself, making possible both the [[Differentiation of Self|preservation of one's own sovereignty]] and the [[Mutual Recognition|recognition of the other's sovereignty]].

A Sotapanna is a first-stage [[Arya|arya]] in whom [[Realization of the Dharma|realization of the Dharma]] has eliminated [[Self-view|self-view]], [[Vicikiccha|doubt about the Dharma]], and [[Clinging|clinging]] to rituals as a means of [[Nirvana|liberation]].

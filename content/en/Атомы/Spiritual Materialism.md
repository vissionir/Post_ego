Spiritual materialism is a form of [[Rat Race|rat race]] in which spiritual practices, ideas, and knowledge directed toward [[Awakening|awakening]], [[Presence|presence]], or [[Disidentification|disidentification]] become a means of strengthening the [[Spiritual ego|spiritual ego]].

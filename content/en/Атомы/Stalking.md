---
title: Stalking
---

Stalking is a behavioral practice of working within the [[Tonal|tonal]] in everyday reality, in which the subject, through [[Reflection|reflection]] and [[Controlled folly|controlled folly]], roles, and strategic behavior, tracks and interrupts [[Mind automatism|automatisms]], ceases to maintain [[Personal history|personal history]], and weakens the [[Sense of Self-Importance|sense of self-importance]], thereby shifting the [[Assemblage point|assemblage point]].

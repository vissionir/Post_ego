---
title: Submodalities
created: 2026-03-05T20:21:28+07:00
---
Submodalities are parameters organizing experience within a sensory modality: brightness and contrast of an image, volume and timbre of a sound, temperature or pressure of a bodily sensation. The term is used in [[NLP]] and describes structural characteristics of experience, changes in which alter subjective feeling. A specific configuration of submodalities forms a [[Submodal state|submodal state]].

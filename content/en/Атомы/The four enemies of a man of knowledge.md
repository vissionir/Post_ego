title: The four enemies of a man of knowledge
---

In [[Carlos Castaneda|Carlos Castaneda]], a man of knowledge has four enemies: [[The first enemy of a man of knowledge|the first enemy]], [[The second enemy of a man of knowledge|the second enemy]], [[The third enemy of a man of knowledge|the third enemy]], and [[The fourth enemy of a man of knowledge|the fourth enemy]]; each enemy arises sequentially and can stop the path if a person takes it for a final attainment.

---
title: The fourth enemy of a man of knowledge
created: 2026-02-07T12:53:43+07:00
---

Old age is the last enemy of a man of knowledge according to [[Carlos Castaneda|Carlos Castaneda]]: in his conception it cannot be defeated, but it can be postponed by continuing the path. [[Transhumanism]] and [[Posthumanism]] regard this limit as potentially surmountable through extending biological life or [[Mind uploading|continuing consciousness on another substrate]].

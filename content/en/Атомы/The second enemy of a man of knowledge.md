---
title: The second enemy of a man of knowledge
created: 2026-02-07T12:53:43+07:00
---

Clarity is the second enemy of a man of knowledge according to [[Carlos Castaneda|Carlos Castaneda]]: it gives a sense of understanding and confidence, but can [[Reliance on Clarity|blind one]], depriving one of caution and doubts about being right, thereby halting progress along the path of a [[Man of knowledge|man of knowledge]].

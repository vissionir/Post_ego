---
title: The unconscious
---
The unconscious is the automatic computational layer of the psyche that keeps matching the present situation to accumulated experience and launches reactions before conscious analysis kicks in. Unlike [[Mind hallucination|mind-generated hallucinations]], it does not fabricate explanations or invent meaning; it reproduces and optimizes behavior according to the parameters of past adaptation, yet it can be [[Congruence|incongruent]] with what is happening now. Almost always it outruns the [[The conscious|conscious layer]].

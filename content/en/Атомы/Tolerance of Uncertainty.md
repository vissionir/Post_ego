---
title: Tolerance of Uncertainty
---

Tolerance of uncertainty is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] in which the absence of [[Predictability|predictability]] or completeness of the [[Map|map]] is accepted as a property of [[Reality|reality]], conditioned by [[Unpredictability of the world|the unpredictability of the world]], and is not experienced as a threat to [[Basic safety|basic safety]].

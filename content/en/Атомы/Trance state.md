---
title: Trance state
created: 2026-02-07T16:12:36+07:00
---

A trance state is a change in the organization of attention in which [[Inner Dialogue|inner dialogue]] may weaken and bodily or emotional perception may intensify, allowing action to [[Wu-wei|unfold naturally]] without constant interference from the [[Mind|mind]]; it makes a direct connection with the [[The unconscious|unconscious]] more accessible and is therefore used in [[Psychotherapy|psychotherapy]].

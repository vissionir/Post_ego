---
title: Trance state
---

A trance state is a temporary shift in attention and experience in which inner dialogue may weaken while bodily or emotional perception intensifies; it makes a direct connection with the [[The unconscious|unconscious]] more accessible and is therefore used in [[Psychotherapy|psychotherapy]]. Trance is neither awakening nor [[Presence|presence]], because it does not end identification with [[Mind|the mind]].

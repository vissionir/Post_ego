---
title: Trance state
created: 2026-02-07T16:12:36+07:00
---

A trance state is a change in the organization of attention in which the character of bodily or emotional perception changes and the sense of conscious control weakens, facilitating the [[Wu-wei|natural unfolding of actions]]; it makes a direct connection with the [[The unconscious|unconscious]] more accessible and is therefore used in [[Psychotherapy|psychotherapy]].

Transition to post-egoism is an [[Irreversible shift|irreversible shift]] arising from [[Letting Go of the Ego|letting go of the ego]], in which the organizing center of existence moves from one's own [[Well-Being|well-being]] to [[Striving for the Good|striving for the good]] of all beings, including one's own good.

---
title: Unconditional Love
---

Unconditional love is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] according to which the subject experiences themselves as the source of [[Love|love]] directed toward themselves and does not make it dependent on conditions; it creates conditions for [[Self-Acceptance|self-acceptance]] because one's own states and aspects do not require conformity to the [[Ideal Self|ideal self]], removing [[Inner Coercion|inner coercion]] toward such conformity and opening [[Inner Freedom|inner freedom]] and [[Wholeness|wholeness]].

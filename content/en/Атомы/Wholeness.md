title: Wholeness
created: 2026-04-27T17:57:29+07:00
---

Wholeness is a state in which parts of [[Selfhood|selfhood]] are not excluded through [[Repression|repression]] but are recognized as "one's own"; as a result, [[Duality|duality]] disappears and [[Ego|ego]] is deprived of its [[Psychic Splitting|foundation]].

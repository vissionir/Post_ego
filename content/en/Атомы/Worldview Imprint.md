A worldview imprint is a form of [[2nd-level imprint|second-level imprint]] in which an [[Implicit Presupposition|implicit presupposition]] becomes fixed in the subject's [[World Picture|world picture]] as the only possibility; it changes through a [[Transition|transition]] to a [[Counter-Presupposition|counter-presupposition]].

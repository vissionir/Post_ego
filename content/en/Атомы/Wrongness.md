title: Wrongness

Wrongness is a [[Presupposition|presupposition]] based on the [[Ideal Self|illusion that a correct version of oneself exists]], in which the subject experiences themselves as wrong, [[Insufficiency|insufficient]], or in need of correction. It dissolves through [[Self-Acceptance|self-acceptance]].

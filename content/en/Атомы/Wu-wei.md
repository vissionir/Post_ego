Wu-wei is following the [[Dao|natural course of events]] through [[Non-doing|non-doing]], in which [[Presence|what is happening is experienced directly]] and actions arise naturally, without unnecessary effort, while their specific form is determined by the [[Mission|mission]]. At the same time, the [[The unconscious|unconscious]] is synchronized with [[Intention|intention]], so action is [[Direct Action|realized directly]], without [[Inner Conflict|inner conflict]].

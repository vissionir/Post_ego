Wu-wei is [[Presence|presence]] in the [[Dao|natural course of events]], in which [[Reality|what is happening]] unfolds through [[Non-doing|non-doing]], without interference from the [[Mind|mind]]; action arises naturally, without the need for [[Inner Dialogue|inner dialogue]] or unnecessary effort, while its specific form is determined by the [[Mission|mission]]. At the same time, the [[The unconscious|unconscious]] is synchronized with [[Intention|intention]], so action is [[Direct Action|realized directly]], without [[Inner Conflict|inner conflict]].

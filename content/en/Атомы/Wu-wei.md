Wu-wei is following the [[Dao|natural course of events]] through [[Non-doing|non-doing]], accompanied by [[Samatha|calm]] and [[Samadhi|collectedness of mind]], in which what is happening is [[Presence|experienced directly]], [[Effortlessness|without a need to hold on to it]], and actions arise naturally, without unnecessary effort, while their specific form is determined by the [[Mission|mission]].

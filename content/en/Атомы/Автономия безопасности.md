---
title: Autonomy of Safety
---

Autonomy of safety is a position of [[Позиция автора|the author position]] in which the subject determines the criteria of what is safe and unsafe for themselves and does not transfer this decision to [[Внешняя легитимация|an external authority]]; it makes [[Ингибирование|inhibition]] possible in relation to [[Тревожность|anxiety]].

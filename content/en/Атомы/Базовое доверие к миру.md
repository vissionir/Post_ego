---
title: Basic Trust in the World
---

Basic trust in the world is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] in which what happens is interpreted as not threatening existence; it is formed in the presence of [[Безусловная любовь|unconditional love]], [[Безусловная ценность|unconditional worth]], and [[Базовая безопасность|basic safety]], is opposed to [[Тревожность|anxiety]], and serves as a basis for [[Присутствие|presence]].

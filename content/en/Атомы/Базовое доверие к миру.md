---
title: Basic Trust in the World
---

Basic trust in the world is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] in which what happens is interpreted as not threatening existence; it rests on [[Базовые пресуппозиции|basic presuppositions]], is opposed to [[Тревожность|anxiety]], and makes possible stable [[Присутствие|presence]] and the [[Позиция автора|author position]].

---
title: Basic Trust in the World
---

Basic trust in the world is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] arising from [[Базовые пресуппозиции|basic presuppositions]], in which [[Реальность|reality]] is interpreted as trustworthy and not experienced as [[Враждебная среда|hostile]]; it is opposed to [[Тревожность|anxiety]].

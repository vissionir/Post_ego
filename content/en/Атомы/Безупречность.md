---
title: Impeccability
---
Impeccability is a way of living and acting with awareness of the [[Контролируемая глупость|conditionality of what is happening]], with full expenditure of energy in every step, without [[Жалость к себе|self-pity]] and [[Чувство собственной важности|sense of self-importance]], taking decisions as final and acting as if each act were the last.

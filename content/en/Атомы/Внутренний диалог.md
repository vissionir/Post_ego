---
title: Inner dialogue
---

Inner dialogue is a verbalized form of thinking through which the [[Ум|mind]] holds, unfolds, and processes the results of [[Предиктивное моделирование|predictive modeling]] in order to coordinate behavior, test scenarios, and make decisions; it is not mandatory and is used as a tool when more precise and consistent work with models is required. Outside a task, inner dialogue indicates an active [[Дефицит|deficit]]. The cessation of inner dialogue leads to [[Остановка мира|stopping the world]].

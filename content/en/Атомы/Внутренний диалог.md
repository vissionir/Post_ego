---
title: Inner Dialogue
---

Inner dialogue is a verbalized form of [[Мышление|thinking]] arising within [[Реальность|reality]], by which the [[Ум|mind]] holds, unfolds, and processes the results of [[Предиктивное моделирование|predictive modeling]] and [[Интерпретация|interpretation]]; it is not an [[Невербальное мышление|obligatory]] form of thinking, and spontaneous inner dialogue is a manifestation of [[Дефицит|deficit]].

---
title: Inner Conflict
---

Inner conflict is a contradiction within the [[Ум|mind]] arising from the [[Дихотомия|dichotomous]] division of what is happening into incompatible alternatives. Instrumentally it is removed in [[Психотерапия|psychotherapy]], and fundamentally it is resolved through direct experience of [[Недвойственность|nonduality]] and [[Таковость|thusness]].

---
title: Encounter with the unknown
---
An encounter with the unknown is the moment when the habitual [[Карта|map]] of perception stops explaining what is happening and a person meets a slice of experience that lies outside their previous description of the world.

It is an irreversible transition: any world-picture, even one absorbed since childhood, is revealed as a description rather than [[Реальность|reality]] itself. From that point on, a fixed interpretation no longer feels final, and [[Реальность|reality]] is experienced differently.

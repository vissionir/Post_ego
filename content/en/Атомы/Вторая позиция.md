---
title: Second position
---

Second position is a way of experiencing in which an [[Эго|inner observer]] appears and a stable mediated relationship to oneself and the world is formed: a person observes, evaluates, and reacts rather than acts directly. In this mode a person is not [[Субъектность|a subject]], interaction with the world is reactive, and the [[Позиция автора|author position]] is inaccessible. Second position is the opposite of [[Первая позиция|first position]].

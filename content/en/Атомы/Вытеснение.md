---
title: Repression
---

Repression is a consequence of [[Отрицание|denial]] in which a part of [[Самость|selfhood]] incompatible with [[Личность|personality]] or the [[Идеальное Я|ideal self]] is excluded from the [[Сознательное|conscious]], repressed into the [[Тень|shadow]], and ceases to be recognized as one's own while continuing to participate in [[Выбор|choice]] and action through the [[Бессознательное|unconscious]]; its exclusion is maintained by [[Цепляние|clinging]] to the opposite pole of the corresponding [[Дихотомия|dichotomy]] as the correct version of the self.

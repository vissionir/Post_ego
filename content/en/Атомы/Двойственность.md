---
title: Psychic Duality
---

Psychic duality is a state of the psyche arising when [[Базовая целостность психики|basic psychic wholeness]] is disrupted, in which one part of [[Самость|selfhood]] is recognized as "I" and another is excluded as "not-I" and displaced into [[Тень|shadow]]; it creates the conditions for [[Эго|ego]] as a partial center of identification.

---
title: Depolarization
---

Depolarization is the effect of [[Интеграция|integration]] of a part displaced into the [[Тень|shadow]], in which the opposite poles of a [[Дихотомия|dichotomy]] cease to be experienced as mutually exclusive and are recognized as parts of [[Самость|selfhood]], whereby [[Цепляние|clinging]] to one of them ceases and [[Целостность|wholeness]] is restored.

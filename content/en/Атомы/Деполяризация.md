---
title: Depolarization
---

Depolarization is the effect of [[Интеграция|integration]] of a configuration displaced into [[Тень|shadow]], in which the opposite poles of a [[Дихотомия|dichotomy]] cease to be experienced as mutually exclusive, [[Цепляние|clinging]] to one of them ends, and [[Свободный выбор|free choice]] becomes possible.

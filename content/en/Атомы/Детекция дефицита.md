---
title: Deficit detection
---

Deficit detection is a function of the [[Ум|mind]] by which it proactively identifies the [[Недостаточность|insufficiency]] of conditions for ensuring safety, [[Предсказуемость|predictability]], and the possibility of action, highlighting a possible [[Дефицит|deficit]] as a priority signal, since [[Достаточность|sufficiency]] does not require a response, while deficit points to potential [[Небезопасность|unsafety]].

---
title: Scarcity mindset
---
Scarcity mindset is a mode of [[Мышление|thinking]] in which the world, resources, possibilities, and the subject themselves are by default experienced as [[Недостаточность|insufficient]], and the absence of the desired is interpreted as a [[Дефицит|deficit]], creating a drive to compensate for the perceived lack and limiting [[Свободный выбор|free choice]]; it is opposite to [[Мышление изобилия|abundance mindset]].

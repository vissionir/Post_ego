---
title: Permissibility of error
---

Permissibility of error is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] according to which, under conditions of [[Непредсказуемость мира|the unpredictability of the world]], a [[Ошибка предсказания|prediction error]] is not interpreted as a threat to [[Базовая безопасность|basic safety]].

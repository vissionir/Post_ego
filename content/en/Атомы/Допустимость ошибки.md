---
title: Permissibility of error
---

Permissibility of error is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] that follows from [[Непредсказуемость мира|the unpredictability of the world]], according to which a [[Ошибка предсказания|prediction error]] is not interpreted as a threat to [[Базовая безопасность|basic safety]] but is used to update and expand the [[Карта|map]].

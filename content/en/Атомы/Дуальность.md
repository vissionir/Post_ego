---
title: Duality
---

Duality is a mode of perception in which unified [[Реальность|reality]] ceases to be experienced as a whole process, and the [[Дихотомия|dichotomies]] of the [[Ум|mind]] are perceived as the very structure of reality; it is opposed to [[Недвойственность|nonduality]] and creates the condition for [[Отождествление|identification]].

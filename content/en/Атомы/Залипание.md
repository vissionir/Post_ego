---
title: Fixation
---

Fixation is a form of [[Цепляние|clinging]] in which attention, thinking, or action become fixed on a single task, state, or line of behavior. For the [[Ум|mind]], this is energetically efficient because [[Энтропия|uncertainty]] is reduced and [[Когнитивный гомеостаз|cognitive homeostasis]] is maintained.

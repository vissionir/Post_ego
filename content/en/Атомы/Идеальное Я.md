---
title: Ideal Self
---

The ideal self is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] based on the [[Иллюзия ума|illusion]] that there exists a fixed "correct" version of the subject relative to which the current state is experienced as [[Неправильность|wrongness]], thereby forming the [[Тень|shadow]]; it is one of the foundations of the [[Ложное Я|false self]] and is opposed to [[Принятие себя|self-acceptance]].

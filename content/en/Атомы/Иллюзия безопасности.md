---
title: Illusion of Safety
---

Illusion of safety is an [[Иллюзия ума|illusion of the mind]] based on the false identity "predictability = safety", as a result of which [[Базовая безопасность|basic safety]] begins to be experienced as dependent on [[Предсказуемость|predictability]]; it is the basis of [[Власть ума|the power of the mind]] and sustains it; the dispelling of this illusion leads to [[Делегитимация ума|delegitimation of the mind]].

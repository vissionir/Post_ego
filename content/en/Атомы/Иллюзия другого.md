---
title: Illusion of the Other
---

The illusion of the other is a [[Иллюзия ума|mind illusion]] in which a model of another person's inner world, constructed by the [[Ум|mind]], is taken to be reality, although the subject is [[Нет никаких людей|factually given]] only that person's manifestations in [[ВАК|VAK]]. The dissolution of this illusion leads to the de-actualization of the [[Внутренняя стая|inner pack]] and leaves the [[Вторая позиция|second position]] only as a method, not as a mode of perception.

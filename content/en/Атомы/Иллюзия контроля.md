---
title: Illusion of Control
---

Illusion of control is a [[Иллюзия ума|mind illusion]] in which [[Контроль|control]] is experienced as a way to make [[Реальность|reality]] sufficiently [[Предсказуемость|predictable]] under conditions of [[Непредсказуемость мира|the unpredictability of the world]]; recognizing it makes [[Недеяние|non-doing]] possible and opens [[У-вей|wu wei]].

---
title: Illusion of Insufficiency
---

The illusion of insufficiency is a [[Иллюзия ума|mind illusion]] in which [[Недостаточность|insufficiency]] identified by [[Детекция дефицита|deficit detection]] is not recognized as a result of the work of the [[Ум|mind]] and is experienced as a property of [[Реальность|reality]] itself; it is the basis of [[Дефицитное мышление|deficit thinking]], and its dissolution leads to [[Трезвость|sobriety]].

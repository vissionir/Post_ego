---
title: Illusion of Lack
---

The illusion of lack is a [[Иллюзия ума|mind illusion]] in which what is identified as missing by [[Детекция дефицита|deficit detection]] is not recognized as a result of the work of the [[Ум|mind]] and is experienced as a property of [[Реальность|reality]] itself; its dissolution leads to [[Трезвость|sobriety]].

---
title: 1st-level imprint
---

A 1st-level imprint is a particular kind of [[Импринт|imprint]]: a fixed bodily-emotional reaction that arose as a result of a specific experience and was fixed at the level of perceptual [[Субмодальное состояние|submodalities]] (tension, contraction, impulse, avoidance, etc.), manifesting as an automatic reaction to certain triggers, but not determining the way of living one’s life as a whole.

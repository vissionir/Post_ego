---
title: 2nd-level imprint
---

A 2nd-level imprint is a particular kind of [[Импринт|imprint]]: a fixation of a way of being in which the current mode of life is experienced as the only possible and objectively real one, choice is not recognized, and exit seems impossible. Work with such an imprint leads not to the correction of a reaction, but to a change in the structure of perception and the restoration of choice.

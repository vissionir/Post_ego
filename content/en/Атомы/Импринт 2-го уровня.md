---
title: 2nd-level imprint
---

A 2nd-level imprint is a particular kind of [[Импринт|imprint]]: a fixation of a modality of living in which the current way of being is experienced as the only possible and objectively real one, choice is not recognized, and exit seems impossible, and work with such an imprint leads not to correction of a reaction but to a change in the structure of perception and restoration of the possibility of choice.

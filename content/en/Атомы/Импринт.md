---
title: Imprint
---

An imprint is a fixed pattern of reaction formed in past experience and automatically activated in the present outside direct perception of reality; in the research, [[Импринт 1-го уровня|1st-level imprints]] and [[Импринт 2-го уровня|2nd-level imprints]] are distinguished, and [[Психотерапия|psychotherapy]] is considered as a process of identifying and eliminating imprints.

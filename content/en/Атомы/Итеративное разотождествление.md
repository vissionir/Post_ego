---
title: Iterative Disidentification
---

Iterative disidentification is a way of weakening [[Отождествление|identification]] in which the subject repeatedly [[Ингибирование|inhibits]] following the [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]] and, through [[ВАК-верификация|VAK verification]], observes that action continues without management by the [[Ум|mind]].

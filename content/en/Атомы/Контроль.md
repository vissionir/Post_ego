---
title: Control
---

Control is the way [[Ум|the mind]] carries out [[Цепляние|clinging]] through an attempt to restore and maintain [[Предсказуемость|predictability]] under conditions of [[Непредсказуемость мира|the unpredictability of the world]]; it is opposite to [[Состояние управления|the state of management]] and serves as a form of [[Власть ума|the mind's power]].

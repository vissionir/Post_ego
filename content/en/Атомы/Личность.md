---
title: Personality
---
Personality is a stable structure of psychic patterns (beliefs, emotional reactions, roles and behavioral strategies, [[Импринт|imprints]], [[Пресуппозиция|presuppositions]], [[Субмодальности|submodalities]], and ways of interpreting experience) that forms a habitual mode of experience and action in the world; it is formed in [[ВАК|VAK]] experience and is therefore a product of environment.

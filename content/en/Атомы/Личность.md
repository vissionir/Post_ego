---
title: Personality
---

Personality is a stable system of psychic patterns (beliefs, emotions, roles, strategies, [[Импринт|imprints]], and ways of interpreting experience) that forms a habitual mode of experience and action.

It arises as an adaptation within childhood [[ВАК|VAK experience]], where a significant adult defines “what reality is,” and is therefore entirely a product of the environment and can be decomposed into elements.

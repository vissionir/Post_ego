---
title: Personality
---
[[Личность|Personality]] is a stable structure of psychic patterns (beliefs, emotional reactions, roles, behavioral strategies, [[Импринт|imprints]], [[Пресуппозиция|presuppositions]], [[Субмодальности|submodalities]], and ways of interpreting experience) that forms a habitual mode of experience and action in the world.

Personality is formed in [[ВАК|VAK]] experience and is therefore a product of environment. As a system of interconnected elements, it can be analyzed and decomposed into components, which makes its full investigation possible. Ego arises as a mechanism of identification with [[Личность|this structure]].

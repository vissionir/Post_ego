---
title: Meta-management
---

Meta-management is a way of [[Состояние управления|self-management]] at the level of [[Пресуппозиция|presuppositions]] and [[Субмодальное состояние|submodal states]], in which the subject, understanding the mechanism of [[Импринт|imprint]], [[Вытеснение|repression]], and the [[Идеальное Я|ideal self]], can use [[НЛП|NLP]] and [[Принятие|acceptance]] to change the current configuration without needing to search for the original [[Импринт|imprint]].

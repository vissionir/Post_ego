---
title: Meta-management
---

Meta-management is a way of [[Состояние управления|self-management]] at the level of [[Пресуппозиция|presuppositions]] and [[Субмодальное состояние|submodal states]], in which direct [[Интеграция|integration]] of parts of the psyche becomes possible without the need to search for and work through [[Импринт|imprints]].

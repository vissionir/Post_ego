---
title: Mission
---
Mission is a system, chosen from the [[Позиция автора|position of the author]], that organizes the way a subject participates in [[Реальность|reality]], sets the [[Картина мира|worldview]], [[Пресуппозиция|presuppositions]], [[Намерение|intention]], and generates current [[Фрейм|frames]] and [[Цель|goals]].

---
title: Mission
---
Mission is a system, chosen from the [[Позиция автора|position of the author]], that organizes the way a subject participates in [[Реальность|reality]], through which the [[Картина мира|worldview]], [[Пресуппозиция|presuppositions]], and [[Намерение|intention]] are formed, and current [[Фрейм|frames]] and [[Цель|goals]] are generated.

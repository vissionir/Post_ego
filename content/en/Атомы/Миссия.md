---
title: Mission
---
Mission is an organizing system of a subject’s participation in [[Реальность|reality]], chosen from the [[Позиция автора|position of the author]], which sets the [[Картина мира|worldview]], [[Пресуппозиция|presuppositions]], [[Намерение|intention]], and generates current [[Фрейм|frames]] and [[Цель|goals]].

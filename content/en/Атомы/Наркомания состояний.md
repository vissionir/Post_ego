---
title: State addiction
---

State addiction is the substitution of awakening and [[Присутствие|presence]] with regularly entering pleasant or unusual states of consciousness, when meditations, breathing practices, yoga, and other techniques are used as a way to obtain a [[Трансовое состояние|trance state]] rather than as a means of exiting identification with [[Ум|the mind]] and [[Эго|the ego]].

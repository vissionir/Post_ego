---
title: Irreversible insight
---
An irreversible insight is an insight after which a person can no longer sincerely return to the former way of interpreting experience. The very [[Точка сборки|point]] from which events are perceived changes.

As a result, the entire perceptual [[Карта|map]] is rebuilt: such an insight cannot be “forgotten” or undone — it changes the structure of [[Личность|personality]] and the way experience is lived.

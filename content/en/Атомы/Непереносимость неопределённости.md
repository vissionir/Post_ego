---
title: Intolerance of Uncertainty
---

Intolerance of uncertainty is a manifestation of [[Тревожность|anxiety]] in which the absence of future [[Предсказуемость|predictability]] is experienced as a threat to [[Базовая безопасность|basic safety]], as a result of which the [[Ум|mind]] launches [[Контроль|control]] to restore certainty.

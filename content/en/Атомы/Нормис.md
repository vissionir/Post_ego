---
title: Normie
---

A normie is a person for whom [[Мышление|thinking]], reactions, and interpretations of [[Ум|the mind]] are experienced as “I” and as [[Реальность|reality]] itself; as a result, they live in [[Автоматизм ума|automatisms]] and their [[Точка сборки|assemblage point]] remains fixed, because [[Реальность|reality]] matches expectations well enough and [[Ошибка предсказания|prediction error]] remains minimal.

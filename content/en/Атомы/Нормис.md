---
title: Normie
---

A normie is a person for whom the thinking, reactions, and interpretations of [[Ум|the mind]] are perceived as “I” and as reality itself, as a result of which they live in [[Автоматизм ума|automatisms]] and their [[Точка сборки|assemblage point]] remains fixed, because reality matches expectations well enough and [[Ошибка предсказания|prediction error]] is minimal.

---
title: Optimism
---
Optimism is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] of the future's [[Базовая безопасность|basic safety]], in which the future is by default interpreted as non-threatening to existence and favorable; it is [[Дихотомия|dichotomous]] to [[Пессимизм|pessimism]] and necessary for a stable [[Позиция автора|author position]].

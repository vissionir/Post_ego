---
title: Optimism
---
Optimism is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] of [[Базовое доверие к миру|basic trust in the world]] with regard to the future, in which the future is by default interpreted as non-threatening to existence and favorable; it is [[Дихотомия|dichotomous]] to [[Пессимизм|pessimism]] and necessary for a stable [[Позиция автора|author position]].

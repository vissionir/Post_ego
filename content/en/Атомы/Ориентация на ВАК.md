---
title: Orientation toward VAK
---

Orientation toward VAK is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] arising on the basis of [[Приоритет реальности|priority of reality]], in which the evaluation of oneself, others, and what is happening is carried out through [[ВАК-верификация|VAK verification]] rather than through declarations and [[Интерпретация|interpretations]] of the [[Ум|mind]].

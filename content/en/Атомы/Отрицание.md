---
title: Denial
---

Denial is an operation of the [[Ум|mind]] in which one side of a [[Дихотомия|dichotomy]] is recognized as inadmissible and excluded from the [[Карта|map]] of experienced [[Реальность|reality]]; it is opposed to [[Принятие|acceptance]] and creates the conditions for [[Внутренний конфликт|inner conflict]] and [[Отождествление|identification]].

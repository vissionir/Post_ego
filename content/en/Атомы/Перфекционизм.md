---
title: Perfectionism
---

Perfectionism is a form of [[Гиперкомпенсация|hypercompensation]] of a [[Импринт 2-го уровня|second-level imprint]] arising from [[Недостаточность|insufficiency]], in which maximum becomes fixed as the only way to restore [[Автономия безопасности|safety]] and therefore becomes the only permissible norm.

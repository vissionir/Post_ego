---
title: Predictability
---

Predictability is a self-sustaining mechanism of the [[Ум|mind]] in which, through [[Предиктивное моделирование|predictive modeling]], [[Негэнтропийность ума|the negentropy of the mind]], and [[Когнитивный гомеостаз|cognitive homeostasis]], what is happening becomes explainable and expected in order to reduce uncertainty, recognize possible danger in advance, and preserve the possibility of further modeling.

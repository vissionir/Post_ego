---
title: Presupposition
---

A presupposition is a basic assumption taken as given and determining the way [[Реальность|reality]] is perceived and interpreted. In the classical formulation of [[НЛП|NLP]], “presuppositions are useful assumptions that we accept and behave as if they were true.” An unrecognized presupposition is experienced not as an assumption but as an “objective fact” and can therefore become fixed as an [[Импринт 2-го уровня|imprint of the 2nd level]] (including at the level of beliefs and identity in the [[Пирамида Дилтса|Dilts pyramid]]).

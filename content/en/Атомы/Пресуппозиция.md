---
title: Presupposition
---
A presupposition is a default assumption that organizes how experience is interpreted; in the [[Пирамида Дилтса|Dilts pyramid]] it belongs to the level of values and beliefs and may solidify at the level of identity, and when it is not chosen and not recognized as an assumption it is experienced as objective inevitability and becomes an [[Импринт 2-го уровня|imprint of the 2nd level]].

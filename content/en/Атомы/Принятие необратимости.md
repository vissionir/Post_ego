---
title: Acceptance of irreversibility
---

Acceptance of irreversibility is the [[Принятие|acceptance]] that [[Необратимость|irreversible]] changes are part of [[Имперманентность|impermanence]]; it ends [[Цепляние|clinging]] to the possibility of returning to a former [[Точка сборки|assemblage point]], thereby eliminating the [[Страх необратимости|fear of irreversibility]].

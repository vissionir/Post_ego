---
title: Acceptance of Mortality
---

Acceptance of mortality is a [[Переход|transition]] in which one's own mortality is recognized as an inseparable and inevitable consequence of [[Энтропия реальности|the entropy of reality]], and thus the preservation of the model of "I" cannot be guaranteed by the [[Ум|mind]], which deprives the [[Иллюзия безопасности|illusion of safety]] of its basis.

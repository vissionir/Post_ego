---
title: Acceptance
---

Acceptance is a position in which the subject does not try to change, suppress, or hold the arising [[Субмодальное состояние|submodal states]], which removes [[Сопротивление|resistance]], as a result of which [[Внутренний конфликт|inner conflict]] disappears; any experience is allowed as part of the process and does not take the subject out of [[Позиция автора|the author position]].

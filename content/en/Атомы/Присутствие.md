---
title: Presence
---

Presence is the direct experience of what is happening “here and now.” In presence, [[Реальность|reality]] is not compared or evaluated, but simply happens; it is not achieved but recognized outside [[Эго|ego]], which is why [[Автоматизм ума|automatisms]] and the [[Власть ума|power]] of the [[Ум|mind]] are absent within it.

---
title: Psychotherapy
---

Psychotherapy is a process of [[Рефлексия|identifying]] and removing [[Импринт|imprints]], through which automatic reactions weaken or disappear and [[Ум|the mind]] becomes quieter as [[Внутренний конфликт|inner conflicts]] are dissolved; psychotherapy also directly participates in restructuring the brain's [[Нейродинамика|neurodynamics]].

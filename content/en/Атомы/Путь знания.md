---
title: Path of Knowledge
---

The Path of Knowledge is the path of the [[Человек знания|person of knowledge]] in which [[Саморазоблачение|self-exposure]] and [[Разрыв отождествления|identification rupture]] are reached through [[Праджня|prajna]]; it does not provide [[Пробуждение|awakening]] itself, because [[Пробуждение|awakening]] is not performed by the [[Ум|mind]].

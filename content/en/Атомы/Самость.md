---
title: Selfhood
---
Selfhood is the integrity of the psyche uniting the [[Сознательное|conscious]] and [[Бессознательное|unconscious]]; what remains itself across changing roles, and the center around which [[Эго|ego]] gathers; in [[Психотерапия|psychotherapy]], it is the node to which externalized parts return through [[Интеграция|integration]].

---
title: Power
---

Power is an inner state of the ability to influence reality and change the course of events; power is one of the key components of the [[Позиция автора|author position]]: without it, intention does not pass into action; in [[Карлос Кастанеда|Castaneda]] power is considered as the [[Третий враг человека знания|third enemy of a man of knowledge]], and in [[Фридрих Ницше|Friedrich Nietzsche]] power is expressed by the concept of “will to power” — the fundamental striving of life toward affirmation.

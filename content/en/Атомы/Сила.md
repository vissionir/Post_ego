---
title: Power
---

Power is an inner state of the capacity to influence [[Реальность|reality]] and alter the course of events; it is one of the key components of the [[Позиция автора|author position]]: without it, intention does not pass into action. In [[Карлос Кастанеда|Castaneda]], power appears as the [[Третий враг человека знания|third enemy of a man of knowledge]], and in [[Фридрих Ницше|Friedrich Nietzsche]], it is expressed in the idea of the “will to power” — life’s fundamental drive toward self-affirmation.

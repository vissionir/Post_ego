---
title: Ego Death
---

Ego Death is a passage through [[Страх смерти|fear of death]] in which [[Разотождествление|disidentification]] occurs as an [[Необратимый сдвиг|irreversible shift]], the need to [[Цепляние за я|cling to "I"]] disappears, and [[Анатта|anatta]] is recognized; it cannot be carried out by the [[Ум|mind]].

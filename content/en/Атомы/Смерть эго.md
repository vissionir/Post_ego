---
title: Ego Death
---

Death of [[Эго|ego]] is a [[Переход|transition]] through [[Страх смерти|fear of death]], in which irreversible [[Разотождествление|disidentification]] occurs, the need to [[Цепляние за я|cling to "I"]] disappears, and [[Анатта|anatta]] is recognized; it cannot be carried out by the [[Ум|mind]].

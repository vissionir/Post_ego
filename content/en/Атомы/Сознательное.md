---
title: Conscious
---

The conscious is the layer of psyche where aware perception and thinking occur; its function is to notice what is happening, analyze the situation, and when needed intervene in automatic reactions of the [[Бессознательное|unconscious]] (i.e., in the regime of [[Автоматизм ума|mind automatism]]). At the same time, most human behavior arises outside this layer: most reactions (up to ~99%) are launched unconsciously, while consciousness often joins after the fact — as a mechanism of correction, choice, and redefinition of an already-started action.

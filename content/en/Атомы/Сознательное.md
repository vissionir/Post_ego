---
title: The conscious
---

The conscious is the layer of the psyche in which aware perception and thinking occur; its function is to notice what is happening, analyze the situation, and, when needed, intervene in the automatic reactions of the [[Бессознательное|unconscious]] (that is, in the regime of [[Автоматизм ума|mind automatism]]). At the same time, most human behavior arises outside this layer: most reactions (up to ~99%) are launched unconsciously, while consciousness usually joins only after the fact, as a mechanism of correction, choice, and redefinition of an action already under way.

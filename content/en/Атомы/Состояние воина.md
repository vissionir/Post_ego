---
title: Warrior state
---

Warrior state is a way of living in which a person takes responsibility for each step and acts without self-pity and personal importance; they do not seek safety, excuses, or guarantees, but act precisely and in time, knowing about their death, and in [[Карлос Кастанеда|Castaneda]] a warrior is a person who refused to be a victim of circumstances and therefore gained freedom of action.

---
title: Sleepers
---

Sleepers are people fully identified with [[Эго|the ego]] and [[Ум|the mind]], living in [[Автоматизм ума|automatisms]] and not aware of the very fact of sleep; they function as NPCs, reacting to the world without choice and [[Присутствие|presence]], and the other extreme is [[Заблудшие|the lost]].

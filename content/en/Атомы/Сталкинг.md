---
title: Stalking
---

Stalking is a behavioral practice of working within the [[Тональ|tonal]] in everyday reality, in which the subject, through [[Рефлексия|reflection]] and [[Контролируемая глупость|controlled folly]], roles, and strategic behavior, tracks and interrupts [[Автоматизм ума|automatisms]], ceases to maintain [[Личная история|personal history]], and weakens the [[Чувство собственной важности|sense of self-importance]], thereby shifting the [[Точка сборки|assemblage point]].

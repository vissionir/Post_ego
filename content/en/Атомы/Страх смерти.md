---
title: Fear of death
---

Fear of death is the ultimate form of [[Страх|fear]] and a key mechanism of fixation of the [[Точка сборки|assemblage point]]: its shift is experienced as a threat to the disappearance of the “I,” so the [[Ум|mind]] continually confirms it through [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]], turning that dialogue into a compulsive process of [[Автоматизм ума|mind automatism]] and holding the [[Эго|ego structure]] in place. Acceptance of death removes the need to maintain the “I”.

---
title: Fear of death
---

Fear of death is the ultimate form of [[Страх|fear]] and a key mechanism for fixation of the [[Точка сборки|assemblage point]]: its shift is experienced as a threat of disappearance of the “I,” so the [[Ум|mind]] continuously confirms it through [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]], turning it into a compulsive process (see [[Автоматизм ума|mind automatism]]) and holding the [[Эго|ego structure]]. Acceptance of the fact of death removes the need to maintain the “I”.

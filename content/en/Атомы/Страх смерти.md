---
title: Fear of death
---

Fear of death is the ultimate case of [[Страх|fear]], ontologically no different from any other, but directed at the disappearance of the “I.” It therefore becomes a key mechanism for fixation of the [[Точка сборки|assemblage point]]: its shift is experienced as a threat to the disappearance of the “I,” so the [[Ум|mind]] continually confirms it through [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]], turning that dialogue into a compulsive process of [[Автоматизм ума|mind automatism]] and holding the [[Эго|ego structure]] in place. Acceptance of death removes the need to maintain the “I”.

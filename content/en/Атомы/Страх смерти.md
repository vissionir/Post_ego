---
title: Fear of death
---

Fear of death is the ultimate form of [[Страх|fear]]: a basic existential experience associated with fear of disappearance and loss of “I.” Any threat is interpreted as a threat to continuity of selfhood and control, as a result of which fear of death holds the [[Эго|ego structure]] and fixes the [[Реактивный способ существования|reactive mode of existence]]. In the tradition of [[Карлос Кастанеда|Carlos Castaneda]], fear of death is considered as the main mechanism of taming a person and maintaining the importance of “I,” and victory over it as a necessary condition for attaining the [[Состояние воина|warrior state]].

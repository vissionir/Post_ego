---
title: Fear
---

Fear is an illusory experience of unsafety arising in the mind and not directly connected with real danger. Fear always has its source in an [[Импринт|imprint]] and does not arise from direct perception of reality. In [[Карлос Кастанеда|Carlos Castaneda]], fear is described as the first of the four enemies of the [[Человек знания|man of knowledge]].

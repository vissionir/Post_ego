---
title: Fear
---

Fear is an [[Иллюзия ума|illusory]] experience of unsafety arising in the [[Ум|mind]] and not directly connected with real danger. Fear always has its source in an [[Импринт|imprint]] and does not arise from direct perception of [[Реальность|reality]]. In [[Карлос Кастанеда|Carlos Castaneda]], fear is described as the first of the four enemies of the [[Человек знания|man of knowledge]].

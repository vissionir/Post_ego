---
title: Submodal state
---

A submodal state is an integral configuration of [[Субмодальности|submodalities]], recognized as an inner state of the [[Ум|mind]]; it determines the way [[Реальность|reality]] is perceived and experienced, and its change automatically restructures interpretation, response, and behavior through a shift in the [[Нейродинамика|neurodynamic mode]].

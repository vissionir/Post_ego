---
title: Assemblage point
---

The assemblage point is the current configuration of perception in which experienced reality is assembled: from what position choice and action are carried out; when the assemblage point shifts, it is not reality that changes but the way it is experienced, and in the ordinary mode it is fixed in [[Ум|the mind]] and the [[Эго|ego structure]], whereas in [[Присутствие|presence]] it is recognized as a configuration and can freely switch or be generated.

---
title: Assemblage point
---

The assemblage point is the current configuration of perception through which experienced [[Реальность|reality]] is assembled: the position from which [[Выбор|choice]] and action are carried out; in [[Эго|ego]] it is fixed by [[Личность|personality]], and when [[Цепляние|clinging]] ceases it becomes [[Нефиксированная точка сборки|unfixed]].

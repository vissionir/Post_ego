---
title: Assemblage point
---

The assemblage point is the current configuration of perception through which experienced [[Реальность|reality]] is assembled — the position from which choice and action are carried out. When the assemblage point shifts, [[Реальность|reality]] itself does not change; what changes is the way it is experienced. In the ordinary mode it is fixed in [[Ум|the mind]] and the [[Эго|ego structure]], whereas in [[Присутствие|presence]] it is recognized as a configuration and can be freely shifted or generated.

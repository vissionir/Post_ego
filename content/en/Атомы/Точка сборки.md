---
title: Assemblage point
---

The assemblage point is the current configuration of perception through which experienced [[Реальность|reality]] is assembled: the position from which [[Выбор|choice]] and action are carried out. In [[Эго|ego]] the assemblage point is fixed, whereas in [[Присутствие|presence]] it is not fixed and can be freely shifted through [[Выбор состояния|state choice]] or generated through [[Генерация конфигурации|configuration generation]].

---
title: Assemblage point
---

The assemblage point is the current configuration of perception through which experienced [[Реальность|reality]] is assembled: the position from which [[Выбор|choice]] and action are carried out. In [[Эго|ego]] the assemblage point is fixed by [[Личность|personality]], whereas the [[Нефиксированная точка сборки|unfixed assemblage point]] can change through [[Выбор состояния|state choice]] or [[Генерация конфигурации|configuration generation]].

---
title: Anxiety
---

Anxiety is a background state of [[Небезопасность|unsafety]] in which threat is experienced as constantly present, while [[Контроль|control]] and defense become the basic mode of responding, triggering spontaneous [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]]; in its stable form, anxiety is a particular case of a [[Импринт 2-го уровня|2nd-level imprint]].

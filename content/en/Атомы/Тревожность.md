---
title: Anxiety
---

Anxiety is a background state of [[Небезопасность|unsafety]] in which threat is experienced as constantly present, attention continuously scans what is happening for signs of danger, while [[Контроль|control]] and defense become the basic mode of responding, and [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]] becomes spontaneous and continuous; it is formed from [[Импринт 2-го уровня|2nd-level imprints]].

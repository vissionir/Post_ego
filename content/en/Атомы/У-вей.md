---
title: Wu-wei
---

Wu-wei is [[Дао|dao]] in action: a mode in which [[Реальность|what is happening]] unfolds through non-doing, without interference from the [[Ум|mind]]; action arises naturally, without [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]] or unnecessary effort. In this mode, the [[Бессознательное|unconscious]] is synchronized with [[Намерение|intention]], so action is realized directly, without [[Внутренний конфликт|inner conflict]].

---
title: Wu-wei
---

Wu-wei is a mode in which what happens unfolds through non-doing — without force or interference from the [[Ум|mind]]. There is no need to control or direct the process, so events arise naturally, directly from [[Реальность|reality]], without [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]] or unnecessary effort. This mode is recognized as [[Присутствие|presence in action]].

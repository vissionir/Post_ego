---
title: Wu-wei
---

Wu-wei is [[Дао|dao]] in action: a mode in which what happens unfolds through non-doing, without interference from the [[Ум|mind]]; there is no need to control or direct the process, so action arises naturally, directly from [[Реальность|reality]], without [[Внутренний диалог|inner dialogue]] or unnecessary effort. In this mode, the [[Бессознательное|unconscious]] is synchronized with [[Намерение|intention]], so action is realized directly, without [[Внутренний конфликт|inner conflict]].

---
title: Friedrich Nietzsche
---

Friedrich Nietzsche is a 19th-century German philosopher who explored the origin of values and the critique of morality, important for this research as a source of understanding [[Сила|power]], [[Воля к власти|the will to power]], [[Сверхчеловек|the overman]], authorship, and affirmation of life without relying on an external source of meaning.

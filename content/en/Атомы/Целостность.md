---
title: Wholeness
---

Wholeness is a state of [[Самость|selfhood]] in which parts of the psyche are not excluded through [[Вытеснение|repression]] but are recognized as "one's own" and cease to be experienced as separate and competing; it rests on [[Базовое доверие к миру|basic trust in the world]], leads to [[Смерть эго|ego death]], and makes [[Снятие разделения|the removal of division]] possible.

---
title: Sense of Self-Importance
---

The sense of self-importance is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] based on the [[Иллюзия ума|illusion]] of the existence of a permanent "[[Эго|I]]" to which the [[Ум|mind]] by default attributes [[Важность|importance]]; as a result, what is happening is interpreted through this center, which fixes the [[Точка сборки|assembly point]].

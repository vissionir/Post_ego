---
title: Sense of Self-Importance
---

The sense of self-importance is a [[Пресуппозиция|presupposition]] according to which there exists an "I" ([[Эго|ego]]) to which the [[Ум|mind]] by default attributes [[Важность|importance]]; as a result, attention becomes fixed on oneself as the center of what is happening and the [[Точка сборки|assembly point]] is held in place.

---
title: Ego
---

Ego is a mechanism of identification with [[Личность|personality]] in which a division into “I” and what is happening arises and an [[Внутренний наблюдатель|inner observer]] is formed. Beyond ego, the figure of the [[Внутренний наблюдатель|inner observer]] disappears and direct [[Присутствие|presence]] arises.

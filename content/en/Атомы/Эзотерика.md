---
title: Esotericism
---

Esotericism is a set of beliefs and practices based on [[Магическое мышление|magical thinking]] and [[Галлюцинация ума|mind hallucinations]], attributing to reality hidden forces, intentions, and laws that cannot be verified through direct experience; esotericism includes notions of “energies,” “vibrations,” karma, destiny, “signs of the Universe,” astral influences, and spiritual entities, and esotericists are [[Наркомания состояний|state addicts]] and belong to the category of [[Заблудшие|the lost]].

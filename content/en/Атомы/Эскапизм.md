---
title: Escapism
---

Escapism is the avoidance of current experience as unsafe, arising from [[Базовая тревожность|basic anxiety]] and directed toward leaving the “here and now” for more predictable or controllable states; it may manifest as the avoidance of actions and states that restore contact with a [[Комплекс|complex]], and it is opposed to [[Присутствие|presence]].

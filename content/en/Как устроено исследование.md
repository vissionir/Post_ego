---
title: How the research works
---

The research is structured as a network of atomic notes (Atoms).

Atomic means logically indivisible: an atomic unit / atomic operation / atomic statement — something that either happens as a whole, or does not happen at all.

An Atom is the minimal indivisible semantic unit of the corpus.

An atomic note is one recorded thought: one distinction, one observation, one conclusion, or one constraint. It is the smallest unit of meaning.

---

A note does not try to explain the whole picture and is not a standalone text.

Meaning in this system is not embedded inside a single note. It emerges from the connections between atomic notes — from how they clarify, constrain, complement, or redefine one another.

That’s why the research cannot be presented as a linear text or a sequential exposition.

The format leans on Zettelkasten logic: knowledge here is not accumulated as “chapters” but assembled as a network. Nodes do not exist on their own, but as part of a configuration.

A single formulation may feel dry or incomplete until it lands in the context of neighboring nodes.

It’s important to understand that understanding here does not arise at the moment of reading, but at the moment when a coherent whole begins to appear.

Individual notes are not meant to be “understood” in isolation. The effect of the research appears when the links between them become visible and can be held.

Notes are written primarily for myself — as working nodes of thinking, not as explanations for a reader.

If a formulation feels unclear or sharp, it only means it needs its context. In that case, it makes sense to open the linked notes and assemble meaning through adjacent concepts, rather than looking for an explanation inside a single text.

Reading assumes navigation, comparison, and returns.

Understanding forms gradually — when the same links begin to repeat in different places and can be held as stable distinctions.

I also do a denser, more interactive layer of work in a closed Telegram channel. There you can ask questions, discuss notes, and follow the development of the research:

https://t.me/post_ego

This format was chosen not for aesthetics, but because it’s the only way I found to preserve the result of the research without distortion: without reducing it to one theory, and without losing the connectedness.

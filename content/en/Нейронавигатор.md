**Neuronavigator** is an AI interface for working with the Post-Ego map.

Neuronavigator is made to make the Post-Ego map easier to work with.

Instead of first having to understand every atom and every connection, you can ask a question directly: about a concept, a state, an imprint, an automatism, or any part of the map you want to understand.

Neuronavigator will find related nodes, explain difficult parts more simply, and show where to go next.

## Available AI chats

[Post-Ego GPT](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6a0d8be1ae548191afe51a435401e106-post-ego-gpt) — Neuronavigator inside ChatGPT.

## How to use it

Write as if you were talking to your own AI chat.

For example:

- “What is ego?”
- “Explain this atom more simply.”
- “Where should I start?”
- “Help me find the imprint.”
- “Analyze my state.”
- “Why does this keep repeating?”
- “Which concepts are connected to this question?”

You do not need to choose the right words. It is enough to describe what you want to understand.

## Privacy

Each user has a personal dialogue with Post-Ego AI.

The Post-Ego team cannot see your messages and does not receive your conversation history.

[OpenAI states](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8554407) that GPT builders cannot view individual conversations users have with their GPTs.

[Anthropic states](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10593882-sharing-and-unsharing-chats) that Claude chats are private by default unless the user creates a share link for a specific conversation.

[Google states](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13743730) that a public link to a Gemini conversation is created only by a separate user action.

无扰是一种[[zh/Atoms/Presupposition|预设]]，源于[[zh/Atoms/Acceptance of What Is Not in Our Power|接纳无法掌控之事]]：主体保持[[zh/Atoms/Inner Freedom|内在自由]]，不再耗费内在资源去[[zh/Atoms/Resistance|抗拒]][[zh/Atoms/What Is Not in Our Power|不在自己掌控之内的事]]，并在[[zh/Atoms/What Is in Our Power|自己能够掌控的范围]]内行动。

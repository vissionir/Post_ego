基本预设是一组根本性的[[zh/Atoms/Presupposition|预设]]，它们构成[[zh/Atoms/Basic Trust in the World|对世界的基本信任]]的基础；包括[[zh/Atoms/Unconditional Love|无条件的爱]]、[[zh/Atoms/Unconditional Worth|无条件价值]]、[[zh/Atoms/Unconditional Neededness|无条件的被需要感]]、[[zh/Atoms/Basic safety|基本安全感]]、[[zh/Atoms/Inner Freedom|内在自由]]、[[zh/Atoms/Self-Acceptance|自我接纳]]、[[zh/Atoms/Self-care|自我关怀]]、[[zh/Atoms/Sufficiency|充足]]、[[zh/Atoms/Abundance|丰盛]]、[[zh/Atoms/Positive Orientation|正向取向]]、[[zh/Atoms/Self-Efficacy|自我效能]]、[[zh/Atoms/Confidence|信心]]、[[zh/Atoms/Noncontingency|非条件依存性]]、[[zh/Atoms/Basic peace|基本平静]]与[[zh/Atoms/Well-Being|安好]]。

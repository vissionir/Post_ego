Sabai-sabai是一种经历[[zh/Atoms/Reality|现实]]的方式，伴随着[[zh/Atoms/Well-Being|安好]]的体验，并以内在放松、舒适、轻松、[[zh/Atoms/Peace|宁静]]、[[zh/Atoms/Serenity|安宁]]、[[zh/Atoms/Unforcedness|不勉强]]、[[zh/Atoms/Effortlessness|不费力性]]和没有[[zh/Atoms/Urgency|紧迫感]]为特征。

战士是一种[[zh/Atoms/Life in the Unknown|活在未知中]]的方式：[[zh/Atoms/Impeccability|无可指摘地]]从[[zh/Atoms/Presence|临在]]行动，不受[[zh/Atoms/Personal history|个人历史]]束缚，保持[[zh/Atoms/Unfixed Assemblage Point|组装点不固定]]，遵循[[zh/Atoms/Intention|意图]]，觉察[[zh/Atoms/Controlled folly|所选之物的约定性]]，并承担[[zh/Atoms/Responsibility|责任]]，不带[[zh/Atoms/Self-Pity|自怜]]与[[zh/Atoms/Sense of Self-Importance|自我重要感]]。
